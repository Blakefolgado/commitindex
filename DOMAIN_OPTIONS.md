## Recommendation

Use **Commit Atlas** at `commitatlas.com`.

It describes a large explorable map of public engineering activity without
limiting the product to one visualization or only GitHub contribution grids.
The `.com` is available at a normal registration price and is clearer in speech
than the `.dev` alternatives.

`Open Office` should remain temporary. It competes in search and naming with
Apache OpenOffice, making the product harder to discover and distinguish.

No domain has been purchased or attached. Availability can change until
registration is complete.
